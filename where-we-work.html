Where We Work

We work in over 70 countries including South Sudan, Syria, Yemen, and South Africa. Open in Maps for directions.

South Sudan

MSF hospital supporting surgical services at Malakal Teaching Hospital.

Syria

Emergency medical response in conflict zones, providing trauma care and vaccinations.

Yemen

Supporting hospitals with medicines, surgical care and malnutrition treatment.

South Africa

HIV and TB care, mental health support, and community health programs.
